## What This Run Does Not Cover

This is not a statewide DCA ingestion. It covers Santa Fe DCA-operated visitor institutions from
the public DCA museum/historic-site family and supporting division pages. Out-of-region DCA sites
such as Los Luceros, Lincoln, Jemez, Fort Stanton, Fort Selden, Coronado, Bosque Redondo, the
National Hispanic Cultural Center, and the Space/Natural History/Farm and Ranch museums remain out
of scope for this Santa Fe-first adapter pass.

## Honest Assessment

This phase improves the Santa Fe corpus modestly but materially: it corroborates the main DCA
museum institutions and adds Vladem Contemporary as a missing campus-level canonical. It does not
change the broad Detour surface dramatically because most DCA institutions were already OSM-known.
The main value is official institutional provenance and clearer conflict visibility around museum
taxonomy, especially `culture` versus `history`.
